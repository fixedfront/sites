The problem

Deductions are 2 to 5 cents of every sales dollar. Most of them go undisputed.

Each dispute takes a person 15 to 20 minutes inside a retailer portal, with the right evidence attached, before the clock runs out. Finance teams file the big ones and let the rest expire.

What we recover

Built on the dispute processes the retailers publish.

Walmart APDP and HighRadius, Target Partners Online and Synergy. We keep the evidence checklist for every deduction code and file each case the way that code wins.

Shortages

Billed-not-shipped, no-merchandise-received, signed-short and concealed cartons, Target A030 carton and A034 unit shortages. Evidence: PODs with DC stamps, carrier-signed BOLs, packing slips.

Duplicate billing and pricing

Walmart code 30 duplicates, price differences and allowance disputes. Small in count, often the largest dollars, and almost always recoverable with both invoices and the PO.

Compliance and performance fines

OTIF, SQEP and Supplier Performance chargebacks. Short 90-day windows, so we watch the current season rather than the back book, and file in the week they land.

Audits and auto chargebacks

PRGX and retailer post-audit claims, Target A176 auto chargebacks. We request the backup first, then treat each one as the shortage or pricing claim it really is.

FAQ

Questions a CFO asks first.

Do you need access to our bank account or hold our money?

No. The retailer repays you on your normal remittance. We invoice our 25% after the repayment shows there, and nothing otherwise.

What do you need from us to start?

For the free scan, one deduction export per retailer portal; that's it. If you go ahead after the report: a two-page agreement, a read-and-dispute login in each portal (Walmart Retail Link / APDP, Target Partners Online / Synergy), a folder of proofs of delivery, and a contact for pricing questions. About two hours in the first week.

How is the scan free?

The scan is how we size the problem and show our work. Many suppliers use it to decide whether deductions are worth anyone's time. If the open dollars are small we will say so, and you keep the report.

Which deductions can still be recovered?

Only those inside the retailer's window: Walmart allows roughly 12 months on shortages and up to 24 months on other accounts-payable codes; Target allows 18 months on invoice-match codes and 90 days on compliance fines. The scan separates open dollars from expired ones so the report shows only what can still be filed.

Will disputing hurt our relationship with the buyer?

Disputes go through the accounts-payable channels the retailers built for exactly this, not through your buyer. We file with the evidence each code requires and never file claims the evidence does not support.

Who does the work?

Deductback is founder-run: Zeluis handles your account from scan to remittance. Matching runs on software; filing, evidence and appeals are done by a person who reads every case.

Can we stop?

Yes, with 30 days' notice. Cases already filed are tracked to a decision and reconciled; the fee applies only to repayments on those cases.

Do you work with retailers other than Walmart and Target?

Walmart and Target first, because together they are most suppliers' largest deduction lines and their dispute processes are published. Tell us which others you sell to and we will say whether we can cover them.
