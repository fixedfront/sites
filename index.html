Questions

Why did I get a preview I didn't ask for?

Because showing beats telling. We only build previews for businesses whose current site is losing them calls, and we only use what you already publish: your name, services, hours, phone and public reviews. If you'd rather we didn't, reply "no" and the preview is deleted the same day.

Do I own the site?

Yes. The content is yours. If you cancel the plan, we hand you the files; you only lose the hosting and the edits service.

What if I need more than one page?

Most service businesses do better with one fast page than five slow ones. If you need a second location or a careers page, it's a $249 add-on.

What happens after I pay?

You get a Stripe receipt and, within the hour, an email from hello@fixedfront.com with two questions: your logo, and anything to change. We apply the edits, connect your domain with you (a five-minute step we walk you through), and the site is live within 48 hours.

Who are you?

Can we talk first?

Sure, ten minutes: book a call. Most clients don't need to; the preview answers the questions.
