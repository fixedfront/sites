What we do

The whole exterior, with a photo report before any work and manufacturer warranties after it.

Roof replacement

New roofs for aging homes and new construction, with manufacturer lifetime warranties on materials.

Roof inspections

A photo report on every component, maintenance suggestions and the roof's remaining life. Routine repairs often done the same visit.

Roof tune-up

Sealant renewed at transitions, loose shingles nailed down, exposed nail heads sealed, with photos.

Storm damage and insurance claims

Storm, leak and sudden-loss inspections, and help with the claim: an accepted vendor for Erie, Farmers, Nationwide, Travelers, The Philadelphia Contributionship and USAA.

Siding

Vinyl, wood and fiber-cement siding, plus repair of storm damage, leaks, soffit, fascia and missing panels.

Seamless gutters

5-inch and 6-inch seamless aluminum gutters with gutter guards in good, better and best options.

Replacement windows

Custom-measured double and triple-pane windows in double hung, casement, sliding, picture, bay and bow styles.

Remodeling and maintenance

Renovation work and the small repairs that stop a small problem becoming a large restoration.
