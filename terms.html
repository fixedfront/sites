The free scan

When you send us a deduction export, we analyse it and return a written report at no charge and with no obligation on either side. The report is an estimate built from the export alone, using the validity weights and dispute windows stated on it; it is not a guarantee of recovery, legal advice, or an audit opinion. We may decline a scan, and you may stop at any time and ask us to delete the file.
