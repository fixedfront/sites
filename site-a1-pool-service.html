What we do

Everything a well-kept pool needs, on a schedule you don't have to think about.

Weekly pool cleaning

Skim, brush, vacuum as needed, empty the baskets. Same day every week, same technician.

Chemical balancing

Chlorine, pH, alkalinity and stabilizer tested and adjusted on every visit, with the readings on your note.

Filter cleaning

Cartridge, DE and sand filters cleaned and inspected on schedule so the pump isn't fighting a clogged filter.

Equipment repair

Pumps, motors, filters, heaters, timers and automation diagnosed and quoted before any work starts.

Green-pool recovery

Neglected or algae-filled pools brought back to clear and swimmable, then moved onto weekly service.

Tile and acid washing

Calcium lines removed from the tile and plaster refreshed when the pool needs a reset.

Also available on request: vacation and rental-property service, pre-sale pool checks, and one-time clean-ups before a party.

What's in a weekly visit

The same checklist every time, so nothing gets skipped on a busy day.

  • Skim the surface and net out debris
  • Brush walls, steps and the tile line
  • Vacuum the floor when it needs it
  • Empty skimmer and pump baskets
  • Test and balance the water
  • Check pump, filter pressure and water level
  • Backwash or clean the filter on schedule
  • Leave a note: readings, work done, anything to watch

Questions people ask

Straight answers before you call.

What does a weekly visit include?

Skimming, brushing, vacuuming as needed, emptying the baskets, testing and balancing the water, a check of the equipment, and a note left for you. The full list is above.

Do I need to be home?

No. Most customers aren't. We need gate access and a clear path to the equipment; that's it.

Can you take on a green or neglected pool?

Yes. Green-pool recovery is quoted separately and usually takes several visits over a week or two before the pool moves onto regular weekly service.

Do you repair pumps, filters and heaters?

Yes. Equipment problems are diagnosed and quoted before any work starts, so you know the cost up front.

What about rain, wind or a party?

Santa Ana winds and winter storms drop a lot into a pool. If yours needs an extra visit, text us; one-time clean-ups before an event are available too.

How do I pay?

Monthly, after the work is done. Ask about payment options when you call.
