Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

What we collect

From prospects: business name, address, phone, website, public reviews and the email address on your site or listing, used to build and send a preview. From clients: your contact details, payment through Stripe (we never see your full card number), the content you give us for the site, and your site's traffic statistics (page views, calls and form submissions, counted without cookies). From visitors to a client's site: the quote form sends what you type to the business; we keep a copy for 90 days for support.

How we use it

To build previews, deliver and support sites, bill the plan and send the monthly report. We do not sell or rent any of it.

Email

Every email we send includes a way to opt out; replying "unsubscribe" removes you within 10 days and we keep your address on a do-not-email list so it stays that way.

Who else sees it

Stripe (payments), Google (Workspace email, hosting analytics), GitHub (hosting), and the AI service we use to draft site text. Each handles data under its own terms.

Your choices

Ask us at hello@fixedfront.com or by mail at 9091 Snowden River Pkwy #1006, Columbia, MD 21046 to see, correct or delete what we hold about you; we answer within 10 business days. Prospect data is deleted 30 days after a preview expires.
